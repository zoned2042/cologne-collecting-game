# Sillage: a fragrance collecting game

Work your way from decant packer to group CEO and build the biggest fragrance collection on Earth, then show it off in a 3D showroom.

**Quickest way to play:** download `sillage.html` and double-click it. It's the whole game in one file and works offline.

To work on the code, open `index.html` (it loads three.js from a CDN). After changing anything, rebuild the single file with `python3 tools/build_single.py`.

## What's in it

- **545 real fragrances from 66 houses**: Afnan, Rayhaan, Lattafa, Armaf, Rasasi, Byredo, Parfums de Marly, Tom Ford, Louis Vuitton, Ralph Lauren (Polo), Valentino, Clive Christian, Creed, Dior, Chanel, MFK, Xerjoff, Amouage, Initio, Kilian, Roja, Nishane and more. It also includes the famous ultra-rare bottles: Clive Christian No.1 Imperial Majesty, DKNY's Golden Delicious Million Dollar bottle and Shumukh.
- **Careers**: 12 jobs, from Decant Packer ($60 a shift) through Software Engineer, Junior Perfumer, Investment Banker, Surgeon, Celebrity Influencer and Creative Director up to Group CEO ($90,000 a shift). Better jobs need shifts of experience, a bigger collection, or courses you pay for (bootcamp, Grasse perfumery diploma, MBA, medical school).
- **Shift minigames**: retail and perfumery jobs give you customers to match with the right scent family. Every other job uses a timing meter where you stop the atomizer in the zone.
- **Signature scent**: wear a bottle from your collection. If its family matches the scent your job rewards, you earn +30%.
- **Boutique**: the full catalog with search, filters, prices that drift daily and eight deals each morning.
- **Vault**: discontinued and legendary bottles. Three live auctions a day against rival collectors, plus a grey market. Vintage bottles gain value over time.
- **Decant desk**: passive income that grows with your collection.
- **Collection**: house completion progress, Clone Wars (owning an original and the well-known bottle inspired by it), batch codes and selling.
- **3D showroom** (Three.js): a rotunda of lit shelves grouped by house with procedural bottles for each house's style. Click any bottle to fly it to the spotlit pedestal, spray it for an atomizer mist, or run the show-off tour, a cinematic walk through your ten most valuable bottles.
- **Unboxing**: every new bottle arrives in its real retail box, modelled from photos of the packaging and sized around the bottle. Louis Vuitton's white cylinder sleeve lifts off a gold-collared base, Maison Christian Dior comes in its white tube, JPG Le Male in its ribbed tin, Khamrah in its lid box over a wood-grain base, and most designer cartons open on a hinged tuck flap (Creed's crest-and-window box, Sauvage, Bleu de Chanel, Eros, Invictus and the rest). Every house in the catalog has its real box, modelled from photos of the packaging. Tap the box, it opens, and the bottle rises out on a fan of light with sparkles. Turn it off with the Unbox button in the header.
- **Your own house**: once you own 10 bottles, found a maison in the Your House tab: name it, pick its city and colours. Compose fragrances from 48 real notes across top, heart and base; pairings form accords (fougère, rose oud, tobacco vanille, smoky pineapple…) and clashes cost harmony. Design the bottle with a live 3D preview: 25 shapes (block, apothecary, orb, faceted gem, pyramid, heart, star, violin, bell jar, decanter, trophy, sculpted torso…), 11 glass finishes (clear, crystal, smoked, colour fade, pearlescent, iridescent, mirror chrome…), 16 caps in 11 finishes, labels or hanging tags, surface details (sailor stripes, ribs, lattice, art deco, pyramid studs, raised crest, metal band) and accessories (tassel, pendant, bow, neck ring, chain, jewel). Then design its box: 9 styles (tuck carton, lift-off lid, slide-off sleeve, book box that swings open, chest with clasp, round, hexagonal or octagonal tube, ribbed metal tin), 19 patterns (damask, Greek key, marble, croc, leather, wood grain, starry night…), an emblem (wax seal, crest, medallion) and matte or foil printing. Name it, price it and launch a first batch. Critics review it, it sells every shift depending on quality, price, reputation and the day's trending family, and the first bottle unboxes onto your own shelf in your own box. Grow with lab, perfumer, factory and boutique upgrades, marketing campaigns and client commissions.
- **Collector requests and mystery boxes**: buyers in the Vault pay over value for bottles on your shelf; the Boutique sells mystery boxes that unbox a random bottle you don't own.
- **Real bottle designs**: all 545 bottles are modelled from photos of the real thing, for example: Lattafa Khamrah and Khamrah Qahwa, Creed Aventus, Green Irish Tweed, Silver Mountain Water and Viking, Dior Sauvage (EDT, EDP, Parfum, Elixir) and Dior Homme Intense, Bleu de Chanel (EDT, EDP, Parfum), Baccarat Rouge 540 and the Extrait, Parfums de Marly Layton, Tom Ford Oud Wood, Tobacco Vanille, Tuscan Leather, Lost Cherry and Neroli Portofino, Afnan 9pm and Supremacy Not Only Intense, Armaf Club de Nuit Intense Man, Byredo Gypsy Water, Louis Vuitton Ombre Nomade, Polo Blue, Valentino Uomo Born in Roma, Clive Christian X, Rayhaan Pacific Aura, Versace Eros, JPG Le Male and Ultra Male, YSL Y EDP, Spicebomb, Lattafa Asad, Acqua di Giò Profumo and 1 Million.
- **Designer and house lines**: the rest of Afnan (9pm, Supremacy, Turathi, Rare), all of Louis Vuitton (Cologne Perfumes and Les Extraits), the Creed millésime range, Maison Christian Dior, Dior Homme, Fahrenheit and Eau Sauvage, Chanel (Allure, Les Exclusifs, Égoïste, Antaeus, N°5), YSL, Armani (Acqua di Giò, Code, Privé, Stronger With You), Versace, Prada, Rabanne (Invictus, Phantom, 1 Million, XS), Jean Paul Gaultier, Givenchy, Dolce & Gabbana, Boss, Burberry, Montblanc, Azzaro, Calvin Klein, Polo, Valentino, Gucci, Bvlgari, Hermès, Guerlain, Cartier, Mugler, Carolina Herrera, Dunhill, Lalique and more. Bottles in one line share a template and pass their own glass, juice, cap and label. Creed Himalaya and 1 Million Parfum are modelled from photos too. Anything without a reference photo falls back to its house's archetype.
- **House bottles**: houses that use one bottle across their line get it for every fragrance (Creed, Byredo, Tom Ford Private Blend, Maison Francis Kurkdjian, Parfums de Marly). Other bottles use a shape in their house's style, and some famous ones show their real glass colour.
- New bottle designs go in `js/bottles.js` (hand-built templates) or `js/bottles-designer.js` (line templates and the generic `Q` builder): one spec per bottle (body shape, glass, juice, cap and artwork).
- Bottle files: `js/bottles.js` (core and hand-built templates), `bottles-designer.js` (designer lines and the generic `Q` builder), `bottles-arabia.js` (Lattafa, Rasasi, Armaf, Rayhaan, The Spirit of Dubai), `bottles-margiela.js` (Replica), `bottles-template.js` (Byredo, Parfums de Marly, Tom Ford, MFK, Clive Christian), `bottles-niche.js` (Xerjoff, Amouage, Initio, Kilian, Roja, Nishane...), `bottles-artisan.js` (Le Labo, Diptyque, Malle, Lutens, Memo, Bond No. 9, Jo Malone...) and `bottles-extra.js` (remaining designer flankers). Boxes are built by `js/boxes.js`; the per-house box designs are in `js/boxes-houses.js`, `boxes-classic.js`, `boxes-designer.js`, `boxes-arabia.js`, `boxes-niche.js` and `boxes-maisons.js`.
- **Sound**: synthesized effects (glass clinks, atomizer spray, register, gavel, box thud, lid pop, tissue rustle) and an ambient showroom score, all generated with Web Audio.
- Progress saves in your browser automatically.

Prices are approximate full-bottle retail in USD and serve as the game's market values.
